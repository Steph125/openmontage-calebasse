# Art direction — Astragale · Encre & Archives

**Design read:** a living herbal scroll. The viewer is not watching a slideshow of paintings;
they are leaning over a real Ming/Song-era page while the ink is still wet.

- **Palette:** rice paper `#EFE6D2`, aged silk `#C9B98F`, sumi ink `#1C1915`,
  vermilion seal `#B8332A`, root ochre `#C8962E` (the astragalus's "yellow" — 黄 of 黄芪).
- **Type:** subtitles in Cormorant Garamond 600 (literary, calm); speaker label in small
  tracked capitals, vermilion. Chinese characters in Ma Shan Zheng (brush script).
- **Motion character:** slow-in / slow-out only, nothing springy. Camera drifts like a
  scholar's eye moving over a scroll (4–8 % scale change per shot). Mist drifts laterally.
- **Transitions:** ink bleed — each new painting spreads out from a point like ink
  soaking into paper (ragged turbulence edge), never a hard cut or a dissolve.
- **Signature device (scarce — 1 beat):** the vermilion seal 黄芪 stamps onto the page the
  moment the master names the plant. Nowhere else.
- **Anti-patterns:** no glossy gradients, no bouncy springs, no emoji, no stock "zen" fonts,
  no fake AI characters, never label a Ming pulsatilla painting as astragalus.
