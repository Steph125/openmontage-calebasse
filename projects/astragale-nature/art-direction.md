# Art direction — Astragale · Nature réelle

**Design read:** a field notebook. Real mountains, real herb, real apothecary — the
camera is a botanist's eye; ink is only the notebook's annotation layer on top of reality.

- **Palette:** natural photography graded cool and slightly desaturated (jade-grey
  mountains), ink black `#141414` for annotations, jade `#5E8C6A` for the master,
  root ochre `#D9A441` for the pupil and for the key word.
- **Type:** Jost (clean, contemporary) for subtitles, white on a soft dark gradient —
  deliberately *not* the literary serif of the ink version. Latin botanical name in Fraunces italic.
- **Motion:** real motion from the video clip; stills get a gentle handheld-like drift
  (subtle x/y sine, 3–5 % zoom), never a pure linear Ken Burns.
- **Transitions:** ink-drop wipe — a ragged black ink stain spreads, then opens onto the
  next shot (dark edge), unlike the paper-bleed of the ink version.
- **Signature device (1 beat):** a brush ensō circle is drawn around the astragalus slices
  while a field-note tag writes itself: 黄芪 · Huáng qí · *Astragalus mongholicus*.
- **Anti-patterns:** no ginseng/ginger stock passed off as astragalus, no pill/capsule
  imagery, no glossy wellness-ad look.
