# Art direction — Calebasse · De la plante au flacon — trois choses comptent (24 s, 720 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/4XmSh6mGp (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** A calm brand statement: grey gridded paper; the magnifier is the only place where the plant is in colour — truth seen up close.

**Palette.** Paper `#F2F0E9`, ink `#1C2622`, forest `#0D3B2E`, lime `#C9EC7A` (end card only).

**Signature device.** The colour-revealing magnifier (grey page, colour lens, 1.5× zoom).

## Honesty rules (binding)
- Product names, origin, preparation and sensations only — no health claim (the product-page descriptions contain claims: never on screen).
- Real Calebasse photos (Cloudinary URLs as served by the site); decor footage from Pexels (free commercial licence, credits in `assets/source/pexels_credits.json`).
- Muet (user decision). Poppins + Ma Shan Zheng subset bundled in `public/fonts`.
