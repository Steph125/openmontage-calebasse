# Art direction — Calebasse · Baume Panacé Rouge — texture (12 s, 360 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/1GukzHSpw (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** One hue, warm and tactile: amber-cinnamon (the real colour of the balm), fast 1.5–2 s cuts.

**Palette.** Amber `#D49A5C`, light `#F2CB93`, deep `#8E4F22`, cinnamon `#5E2C12`, cream `#FFF4E6`.

**Signature device.** A procedural balm relief (SVG lighting on displaced noise) — crisp at any scale, used as the world the tin lives in.
