# Art direction — Calebasse · Fleurs à infuser — la gamme (20 s, 600 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/2iRiCFRDL (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** A range film: four flowers, four colour worlds, the same kraft bag — joyful and generous, never shouty.

**Palette.** Rose magenta `#E2427F→#6E0D37` · Osmanthus gold `#F4B43A→#9A5506` · Camomille citron `#F6E486→#A88A1C` · Jasmin jade `#3EA47F→#0B4433` · finale forest `#0D3B2E`.

**Signature device.** A cream seal with the flower's Chinese name brushed vertically (玫瑰花 / 桂花 / 杭菊花 / 茉莉花), and staggered colour curtains between worlds.

## Honesty rules (binding)
- Product names, origin, preparation and sensations only — no health claim (the product-page descriptions contain claims: never on screen).
- Real Calebasse photos (Cloudinary URLs as served by the site); decor footage from Pexels (free commercial licence, credits in `assets/source/pexels_credits.json`).
