# Art direction — Calebasse · Racine de ginseng — l'allée (13 s, 390 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/59sJWLdvp (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** Hushed and noble: an endless sage corridor of identical jars; light at the end; the king arrives in focus.

**Palette.** Sage gradient `#355446→#A9C2AE→#1E3428`, ivory text `#F4F7F1`.

**Signature device.** Hand-projected 3D corridor (perspective, depth-of-field blur, atmospheric haze, floor reflections) + real smoke footage.
