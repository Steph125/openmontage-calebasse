# Art direction — Calebasse · Baies de goji premium — le socle (10 s, 300 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/3cn23RezE (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** Sunny and simple: summer sky, plaster plinth, moving foliage shadows, a hand presenting the bag.

**Palette.** Sky blue, plaster `#ECE7DE`, goji red `#C8341F`, forest `#0D3B2E`.

**Signature device.** Real foliage shadows extracted from a photo, confined to the plinth and the bag (alpha-masked).
