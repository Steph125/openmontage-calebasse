# Beat plans (shared by the Remotion and HyperFrames builds — same timings, same art)

30 fps · 1080×1920 · one locked shot. Each beat's *primary subject* differs from the one before.

## Concept A — « Le chantier du Green Friday » (12 s = 360 f)
| # | frames | primary subject | what happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| A1 | 0–24 | the crew + covered phone | Establishing: black BLACK FRIDAY banner hangs over the phone, held by the two ladder workers; banner breathes. Roller worker crouches into his paint tray (anticipation). |
| A2 | 24–100 | the roller stroke | Roller pole swings up in an arc; a lime bar is rolled through "BLACK" at −7°, two drips run. |
| A3 | 100–175 | the brush lettering | Brush worker raises his pole and letters "GREEN" above, left→right, brush tip leading; "GREEN" pops (overshoot). |
| A4 | 175–200 | the ladder workers | They look at each other, nod, lift the banner 10 px (anticipation). |
| A5 | 200–245 | the falling banner | Released (left 3 f before right): gravity fall, folds compress into a pile, squash + settle. Phone screen revealed top→bottom. |
| A6 | 245–305 | the phone page | Green Friday hero (Black barré → Green, "Jusqu'à -70 %") then smooth scroll to 4 real products; pills pop staggered; reference prices get struck. |
| A7 | 290–335 | the cleanup gag | Ground workers grab the black pile and drag it off-frame right ("on range le Black Friday"). |
| A8 | 315–360 | end card | Logo + « Green Friday · du 20 au 30 novembre » + calebasse.com; fine print. Ladder workers wave. |

## Concept C — « Les ouvriers remplissent la vitrine » (15 s = 450 f)
| # | frames | primary subject | what happens |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | 0–30 | the empty page | Phone shows the Green Friday page: hero + 6 empty dashed slots ("vitrine en travaux"). Crew waits; one ladder worker taps the screen. |
| C2 | 30–120 | the first carry | Worker walks in from the left with a product tile on his shoulder (jujube), hands it up to the ladder worker who slots it: tile snaps in, pill pops, price drops in. |
| C3 | 120–260 | the relay | Rhythm builds: tiles arrive from both sides, passed up and slotted (mandarine, coffret Voyage en Chine, goji, coffret Découverte, coffret Beauté), each with its real pill + struck price. |
| C4 | 260–320 | the full shop window | Last tile slots; grid complete; the crew steps back; the hero's "Black" gets its lime strike + "Green" (signature echo). |
| C5 | 320–390 | the inspection | Ladder worker polishes the screen with a cloth (shine sweep); ground workers high-five. |
| C6 | 390–450 | end card | Logo + « Green Friday · du 20 au 30 novembre » + calebasse.com; fine print. |
