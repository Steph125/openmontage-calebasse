# Art direction — Calebasse · Poudre de perle — la source (16 s, 480 f)
Reference: https://pin.it/11ASPTq0T (analysed keyframes in `reference/`).

**Design read.** Serene and mineral: water is the origin of the product (freshwater pearls).

**Palette.** Stone beige `#E9E4DA`, deep sage `#24332B`, forest `#0D3B2E`, mist `#DCE7E0`.

**Signature device.** A drawn iridescent pearl that travels through the film; water beads masked to the jar.

## Honesty rules (binding)
- Product names, origin, preparation and sensations only — no health claim (the product-page descriptions contain claims: never on screen).
- Real Calebasse photos (Cloudinary URLs as served by the site); decor footage from Pexels (free commercial licence, credits in `assets/source/pexels_credits.json`).
- Muet (user decision). Poppins + Ma Shan Zheng subset bundled in `public/fonts`.
