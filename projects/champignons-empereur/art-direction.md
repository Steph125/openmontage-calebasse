# Art direction — Les champignons de l'Empereur (君臣佐使)

**Design read:** "Coupe de sous-bois". We look at the world the way a mycologist does — in
cross-section. Humus-dark ground, ivory mycelium that *grows* between ideas (the connective
tissue of the whole film), specimen cards like a naturalist's cabinet, and a finale where the
mushrooms become an imperial court held together by threads.

- **Palette:** humus `#17120E`, soil strata `#2A1F17 / #3A2B1F`, mycelium ivory `#EFE6D2`,
  spore gold `#D8B36A`, reishi lacquer `#8E2A1E → #C4552B`, seal vermilion `#B8332A`,
  winter sky `#8FA3B5`, summer sky `#E6B866`, cordyceps stroma `#6B4A2B`.
- **Type:** Fraunces (organic, slightly soft serif) for titles & specimen names; Jost for
  captions; Noto Serif SC for specimen hanzi; Ma Shan Zheng brush for the hook and seals.
- **Motion:** growth is the verb. Hyphae branch procedurally (deterministic L-system),
  stroke-drawn; stalks push up with slow-in/fast-out; cards slide like drawers; seals stamp
  (overshoot + thud). Nothing crossfades without something growing across the cut.
- **Signature device (1 beat):** the four imperial seals 君 臣 佐 使 stamp into place and
  golden mycelium pulses from the Emperor to each minister.
- **Claims:** traditional uses only; no brand, no product, disclaimer on the end card.
