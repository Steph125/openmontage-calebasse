# Art direction — L'horloge des organes (子午流注)

**Design read:** "Nocturne du Qi". It is 3 a.m.; the screen is the night itself. Qi is the only
light source — thousands of gold motes that *are* the story: they form the time, become a
river, close into the 24-hour wheel, breathe as lungs, then return to the wheel.
Everything is generated in code — no stock, no stills.

- **Palette:** night ink `#05060A → #0B1020`; Qi gold `#F3C969`; five-element accents:
  Bois `#4FBF7F` · Feu `#E8553A` · Terre `#E0A93E` · Métal `#DCE4EE` · Eau `#4C7FE0`.
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond 600 caps for hero organ names (letters resolve from blur);
  Jost 500 for karaoke captions; Ma Shan Zheng brush for hanzi.
- **Motion language:** particles morph between formations with per-particle stagger + swirl
  (never a crossfade); camera is 3D (wheel tilts up from 62° to flat), pushes toward the
  organ being named, dives into the lung sector, pulls back at the end. Springs only on the
  clock hand (mechanical overshoot); everything else slow-in/slow-out.
- **Sync:** every visual event is locked to Whisper word timestamps (organ blooms land on
  the organ's name, the midnight–noon axis strikes on "midi-minuit").
- **Signature device (1 beat):** the midi-minuit law — a diameter strikes across the wheel,
  one organ flares while its opposite dims like a scale, then all six axes draw a spinning star.
- **Sound:** fully synthesized — drone bed, singing bowls tuned per element, clock ticks,
  whooshes, a needle "ting", a falling drop. License-free.
- **Anti-patterns:** no anatomical gore, no stock wellness imagery, no medical claims
  ("la tradition y lit…"), no crossfade slideshow.
