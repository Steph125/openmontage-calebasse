# Art direction — Rituels d'automne (秋)

**Design read:** "Le carnet d'automne". The inverse of the organ-clock nocturne: daylight,
paper, air. A calm hand draws one simple thing per ritual; watercolour washes breathe in
behind it; a few ginkgo leaves drift. Lots of negative space — one idea per screen.

- **Palette:** paper `#F4EEE3`, ink `#2B2621` (thin 3 px lines), ginkgo ochre `#D9A54A`,
  rust `#B5522B`, sage `#9AAE8C`, dusk blue `#7C8FA8` (ritual 1), metal grey `#AEB6BF` (ritual 2).
- **Type:** Cormorant Garamond 300/500 (huge thin ritual numerals, titles); Jost 400 small
  captions in ink, phrase by phrase (no karaoke). Noto Serif SC for hanzi, light and quiet.
- **Motion:** everything is *drawn* (stroke-dashoffset on pathLength=1) or *settles*
  (slow ease-out, no springs, no bounce). Leaves fall on a sway (sin x + rotation).
  Section changes: content fades and lifts 24 px while a new wash blooms — never a cut.
- **Signature device:** the ginkgo leaf — draws itself in the opening, becomes the
  corner motif, is shed by the branch in ritual 4, and a single leaf lands at the end.
- **Sound:** original pentatonic plucked strings (Karplus-Strong) + soft pad; a real
  "sss" exhale for the breath ritual; chime on each ritual number; leaf rustle.
- **Anti-patterns:** no particles/glow (that was the night piece), no dark background,
  no stock photos, no medical claims.
